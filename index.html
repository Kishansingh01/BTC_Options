Technical Walkthrough Questions & Architectural Answers

1. How does NautilusTrader start and initialize?
Orchestrated by TradingNode. node.build() sets up the LiveClock, MessageBus, LiveCache, Portfolio, and the Live Data and Execution Engines. Strategies are registered with these facades, and node.run() boots the event loop and exchange connections.
2. How have you configured the Bybit integration?
Configured via BybitDataClientConfig (product types: Option and Linear for BTC reference) and BybitExecClientConfig (product type: Option for margin derivative execution) across Testnet/Demo/Mainnet.
3. How does NautilusTrader receive market data?
Receives real-time JSON via Bybit WebSocket endpoints, parsed via zero-copy Rust/Cython bindings into typed QuoteTick / TradeTick events, dispatched across the MessageBus to on_quote_tick().
4. How are BTC option instruments identified and subscribed to?
BybitInstrumentProvider loads active options. find_atm_straddle_pair() groups by expiry, filters pairs with both Call & Put, and minimizes $|K - P_{\text{ref}}|$ against the BTC reference price.
5. How does the strategy receive market data events?
Via on_quote_tick(tick: QuoteTick). Underlying ticks trigger strike selection; Call & Put ticks update ask prices for continuous buyback valuation.
6. How are orders submitted to Bybit?
Constructed with self.order_factory.market(...), submitted via self.submit_order(order), validated for risk by LiveExecEngine, HMAC-SHA256 signed, and dispatched to Bybit trade streams.
7. How does the system know whether an order was accepted or executed?
Through state machine events: on_order_submitted, on_order_accepted (venue acknowledged), on_order_rejected, and on_order_filled (fill details with price, quantity, and fee).
8. How are positions tracked?
Managed automatically in NautilusTrader's Portfolio subsystem, updating average open prices and emitting on_position_opened, on_position_changed, and on_position_closed.
9. How have you implemented Stop-Loss and Take-Profit?
Calculated using ask-side buyback quotes ($\text{Call Ask} + \text{Put Ask}$). Stop-Loss triggers at $+1\%$, Take-Profit triggers at $-2\%$. Guarded by atomic flag is_exiting = True and executed with reduce_only=True.
10. What happens when one leg gets executed but the other leg does not?
Defensive leg failure handling: immediately cancels the working partner order and submits an emergency closing order with reduce_only=True on the filled leg to prevent naked directional delta exposure.
